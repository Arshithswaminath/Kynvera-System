Raise a ticket by email

Anyone can raise a work order by sending an email to the address below — no Injaaz login required. The system reads the email and creates a draft ticket; a supervisor then reviews it under Draft Tickets and converts it into an active work order. Following the format below helps the system fill in more fields automatically, but nothing is required beyond a subject and a message — anything missing is simply filled in during review.

{{ intake_email }} 
[Project Name] Category - Priority - Short title
Priority: low / medium / high / critical. Any part you skip is just left blank for review. 
Property: Tower A
Zone: Ground Floor
Unit: Shop 4

Describe the issue here in as much
detail as possible. Attach photos
directly to the email if you have any.

Full example

To: {{ intake_email }}

Subject: [Tower A Residential] Plumbing - High - Leaking pipe under sink

Property: Tower A
Zone: Ground Floor
Unit: Shop 4

There is a leaking pipe under the kitchen sink, water pooling on the floor.
Attached two photos.
