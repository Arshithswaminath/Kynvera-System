Who this applies to

This page describes the operations platform running on this host. Your organisation is the controller of staff, contractor, and site records held here. Kynvera provides the software and hosting used to process that data on the organisation’s instructions.

Depending on which modules you can access, the workspace may hold names, work emails, phone numbers, job titles, project assignments, login and audit logs (including IP address and timestamps), signatures captured on device, photos from inspections, HR form contents, and similar operational records.
