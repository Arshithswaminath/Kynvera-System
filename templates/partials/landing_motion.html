FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need a separate login for each application?

No. One Kynvera account signs you into the hub, and launching Fire System or Ajman Municipality hands your session across automatically.

Can I control who sees which application?

Yes. Each account carries entitlements per application, so an administrator decides exactly which teams can open which product.

Does it work on site, from a phone?

The hub and both applications are built mobile-first, so inspectors can capture work in the field and it appears in the office immediately.

What happens to our existing records?

Existing data stays in place. Applications are added to your hub one at a time, and history remains attached to the same assets and sites.

Is there an audit trail?

Every submission, review and approval is recorded with the user, the timestamp and the stage, so nothing changes without a trace.

How do we get started?

Send us a note about how your teams work today. We set up your workspace and grant application access, and most teams are running their first workflow the same day.
