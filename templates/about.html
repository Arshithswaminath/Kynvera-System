Who we are

Fire safety and security, expertly delivered

Kynvera has taken the fire protection and security industry in Ajman to a whole new level. We design, supply, install, commission, and maintain integrated fire and security systems for commercial, residential, and industrial properties across Dubai and Ajman.

From the first risk assessment through to ongoing maintenance, every system is delivered to NFPA standards and approved by Dubai and Ajman Civil Defence—so compliance and safety are never in question.

End-to-end delivery

Design, supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance under one accountable team.

Code-compliant by design

Every system meets NFPA standards and Civil Defence approval across Dubai and Ajman.

Integrated fire & security

Detection, protection, CCTV, and access control engineered to work together.

Managed service

Proactive maintenance and 24/7 support keep your systems ready when it matters most.

Why it matters

Safety is our first priority

When fire and security systems are designed, installed, and maintained properly, risk drops and confidence rises. We protect the people and assets our clients value most.

Kynvera combines certified expertise with a relentless commitment to quality—delivering protection you can trust, every single day.
