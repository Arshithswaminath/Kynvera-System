Settings

Purchase request emails

Four messages follow a request from quotation to invoice. Nothing sends until you add a To address and save.

How to set this up

Do these once. After that, emails follow the purchase request.

  1. 1
    Put real addresses in To

    Comma-separated. Leave To empty and that email is skipped. CC is optional.

  2. 2
    Keep the approve link on the two approval emails

    Leave {approve_url} in the body so GM or finance can approve from the message. They can also open the request in Procurement and review both PDFs there.

  3. 3
    Save each card

    Click a placeholder below to insert it into the subject or body. Use Attach PDF if that email should include the related file. Reset default restores the standard wording.

When they send

Matches the steps on the purchase request.

  1. 1
    Send for approval

    Procurement has uploaded quotations and clicks Send for approval.

    Email: Quotation ready for GM / finance
  2. 2
    GM or admin reviews

    They open the email link, or open the request and check the purchase-request PDF then the quotation, then approve.

  3. 3
    Quotation approved

    Both PDFs are stamped. Notify procurement or the requester.

    Email: Quotation approved
  4. 4
    Goods received, then invoice

    After receive, upload the supplier invoice. GM or system admin must approve it. Once stamped, the request is closed.

    Emails: Invoice for approval, then Invoice approved
